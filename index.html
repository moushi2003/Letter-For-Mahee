TO THE ONE I'LL ALWAYS ADMIRE FROM A DISTANCE...

The Things I Never Said

♡

First of all… pardon me for falling in love with you.

I never intended for my heart to choose you. I don’t even know when it happened. Somewhere between our conversations, your voice, your laughter, the little things you did, and the comfort of simply having you around, you became someone my heart quietly chose.

I tried so hard to move on. But every time I tried to walk away, somehow it only taught me to love you even more.

I know you will never be mine, and I have accepted that reality. So from now on, I will love you the only way I know how— silently, from a distance, without asking for anything in return.

Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for giving my heart a feeling I never thought I would ever be able to feel.

You probably never knew it, but you saved me.

You thought we were just talking. I was surviving.

You thought your messages were ordinary. To me, they became reasons to keep going.

You thought you were simply being a good friend, but you gave me something I had been missing for years— hope, safety, and a reason to believe that good people still exist.

You never knew how much your presence meant to me. But I will always be grateful for it. Sometimes, the people who save us never realize that they did.

I wish I could explain what you mean to me. I wish words were enough.

It’s the way my heart calms down when I hear your voice. The way your presence can make everything feel lighter after the worst day. The way even ordinary moments with you somehow become precious.

You became my safest place, my favorite feeling, and one of the first thoughts on my mind every day. You entered my life so normally, so unexpectedly... yet loving you became the most natural thing in the world.

I love the little things about you the most— the way you talk, the way you laugh, your smallest habits, the random conversations, the teasing, the late-night talks, the emotional moments, and even the silences.

Sometimes I reread our conversations with the biggest smile on my face, because I still can’t believe I found someone like you. Someone who could understand me without me having to say much. Someone whose presence alone could calm me down.

You don’t even realize how much happiness you brought into my life, how much comfort your voice gave me, or how loved I felt simply because it was you.

Maybe this was worth fighting for.

You were the first person I truly wanted to try with after my biggest heartbreak. Not because you were perfect, but because for the first time, I looked at someone and thought, “Maybe this is worth fighting for.”

I was ready to be patient. I was ready to grow together, to choose you even during difficult times, and to build something real— something that could last.

I gave you the most honest version of my heart, hoping you would see how genuine my love was. Maybe you never realized how deeply I cared, or how willing I was to risk everything just to build a forever with you.

But sometimes, the people we choose with our whole heart never realize how deeply they were loved.

I know my efforts may never have been enough for you. Forgive me if I never knew how to show it properly. I don’t know how much I will ever be able to do for you, but whatever I can, I will.

Maybe I was a little too attached. Maybe sometimes my love felt like too much. If it ever overwhelmed you, I’m sorry.

I never meant to make you feel trapped or burdened. I just loved you the only way I knew how— fully, honestly, and sincerely.

Maybe I was trying to give you the kind of love I once wished someone had given me: the kind that stays, listens, cares, and doesn’t walk away too easily.

If my love ever felt heavy, please know that it always came from a soft and genuine place.

It was my choice to wait, so I will never blame you for it.

Even after realizing there was no chance of us being together, this foolish heart still wanted to wait. And perhaps that is the hardest part— to know there is no future together and still have a heart that refuses to stop hoping.

But I know my limits, and I respect your boundaries.

Yes, I like you.

More than I should, perhaps.

But I will stay quiet and admire you from a distance, until one day I either find the courage to finally say everything… or find the strength to let you go.

There’s no point in telling you. If I say those three words, I already know your answer. And perhaps after hearing it, neither you nor I would ever be the same again.

Sometimes I wonder— what if I never stop dreaming about you?

At night, you are mine in my dreams. But when I open my eyes in the morning, you’re gone.

I don’t even know what I feel until I pick up a pen and write about you. When it’s about you, the words just keep flowing.

All I have sometimes is this love and a pen that knows too much about you.

My diary is full of you.

It is filled with all the things I could never say, all the feelings I was too afraid to reveal, all the moments I wanted to hold onto forever.

So maybe I shouldn’t tell you.

Maybe some feelings are meant to remain quietly written between the pages of a diary rather than spoken aloud.

Because I don’t want my love to become something that makes you uncomfortable. I would rather carry it silently than make you feel like you owe me something in return.

If I ever let you go...

If I ever stop talking to you or remove myself from your life, I hope you understand how difficult that decision was for me.

I have a bad habit of holding onto the little bit of good in people, no matter how much they hurt me.

So if one day I stop caring, it won’t be because I suddenly stopped loving you.

It will be because I finally reached my limit.

And maybe that is what love means for me now— not possession, not expectation, not forcing someone to stay.

Just caring.
Just wishing you happiness.
Just being grateful that, for a little while, our paths crossed.

I don’t need perfect days or grand gestures. I only wanted something real. Something that felt safe. Something that lasted.

But even if I never get that with you, I will still be thankful that I met you.

Because meeting you taught me that true love isn’t always about having someone.

Sometimes, it is about wanting the best for them even when you know they may never choose you.

I only pray that life gives you every happiness and success you deserve.

And if you ever feel like no one is proud of you, remember that somewhere out there, someone will always be praying for your happiness— even if you never find out who.

I hope you are happy whenever you think of me.

Because I am happy whenever I think of you.

No matter where life takes us, I hope one thing never completely changes— the care, the understanding, and the beautiful memories we shared.

You are genuinely one of the best things that ever happened to me.

And if I had to choose again, in every lifetime, in every version of life…

I think a part of me would still choose you.

Maybe this message will remain unsent forever.

Maybe you will never know how much you were loved.

But one thing will always remain true:

My feelings for you were never a show.

Whatever it was, it came straight from my heart. ❤️‍🩹

And lastly…

With me or without me, I hope Allah takes care of you, no matter what.

If I could give you one thing in life, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes— because only then would you realize how special you truly are to me.

You were never just my favourite person.

You became a feeling, a memory, a comfort, a chapter of my life that I will carry with me for a very long time.

And perhaps, somewhere in this world, there will always be a heart quietly wishing for your happiness. Mine.

♡

Some feelings are never meant to be spoken.
